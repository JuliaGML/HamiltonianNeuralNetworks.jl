HamiltonianNeuralNetworks

HamiltonianNeuralNetworks learns Hamiltonian vector fields from data. Its network approximates the Hamiltonian, and the vector field is the symplectic gradient of the network [1].

The package provides

  • the architecture StandardHamiltonianArchitecture,
  • the losses HNNLoss, for vector field data, and SymplecticEulerLoss, for pairs of states one timestep apart,
  • the SymbolicPullback of HNNLoss, a gradient that SymbolicNeuralNetworks generates in closed form.

The networks train with the DataLoader, Batch, train! and the optimisers of GeometricMachineLearning; the tutorial shows the whole workflow.
